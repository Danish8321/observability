Neutral observability plan review

Revision 6 of the plan, read against the code on ccr-262b47b0-gflgj5, docs/diagnostic-queries.md and the panel build table in docs/onboarding/backend-and-dashboards.md.

Verdict

Will it give proper observability?
Yes, as of revision 6

Revision 5 made the library reusable and safe but added little for incidents. Revision 6 adds detection (alerts by email, a dead man's switch, SLO burn-rate alerts) and diagnosis (tail sampling, links between traces, logs and metrics, exemplars, runbooks). At the end of phase 1, 24 of the 30 dashboard panels have data, up from 15 today.

Is it achievable?
Yes, but slowly at current capacity

Everything is buildable on OpenTelemetry 1.17 and the pinned collector, with six facts still to verify, each with a fallback. Phase 1 is about 23.5 engineer-weeks. At the recorded 0.25 FTE that is about 22 months. Alerting arrives first, after about 5 engineer-weeks: roughly 5 months at that pace.

The six facts are: whether a metric view can deny keys; the exemplar default; how to detect a prior OpenTelemetry registration; which .NET propagator carries baggage; whether the collector's OTTL reaches exemplar attributes; and whether it can re-aggregate metric points. In an earlier reply I said alerting would land "within the first two months". That holds at full time; at 0.25 FTE it is about five months.

What revision 6 still leaves open

Gaps

  • Retry count (3.4) needs a counter or the JetStream redelivery count. A small addition to M10.
  • Disk and query latency (4.6, 4.7) have no metric chosen yet. Tail sampling makes collector memory and disk matter more.
  • Backup restore (4.8) is an infrastructure task outside this repository.
  • Grafana watching itself cannot report its own death. The daily heartbeat email is the only cover, and it relies on someone noticing a missing email.

Risks

  • Capacity. At 0.25 FTE, phase 1 runs close to two years. More time, or an accepted slower pace, needs deciding now.
  • Thresholds are guesses. There is no traffic baseline (ADR-0027). Expect to retune after two weeks.
  • Tail sampling holds spans in collector memory and needs one collector per trace; more than one instance needs a trace-aware load-balancing tier.
  • Production email needs an SMTP server and a recipient list before go-live; the demo uses a local mail catcher.

Recommended next steps

  1. Decide capacity. Choose between more than 0.25 FTE and the roughly 22-month pace before M2 starts.
  2. Add a retry counter to M10 so panel 3.4 is complete. Choose disk and query-latency sources for 4.6 and 4.7 while writing M9.
  3. Split the plan. Move contracts C1–C5 into ADRs 0032–0040 as M0 writes them, and keep the plan as a one-page index with milestones and status.
  4. Start M0 and M1 now. They are small and independent, and M1 fixes a live data leak.
